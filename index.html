WMH Rater Trainer

Paint white matter hyperintensities on FLAIR MRI, compare with the expert and with the algorithm
How to
  1. Paint every white matter hyperintensity (WMH) you see with the brush. Use the eraser to correct and zoom in for small spots.
  2. Check every slice (thumbnails under the picture), then press Score.
  3. Compare your mask with the expert's and with the algorithm (BIANCA), then go on with Next patient, as long as you like.

What to mark: on FLAIR, WMH are bright spots in the white matter, around the ventricles and in the deep white matter. Mark only WMH, no other lesions.

Do not mark: the bright cortex, the choroid plexus inside the ventricles, artefacts. Tricky: the thin bright rim along the ventricles and the caps at their tips.
