The files in `data/`, the screenshots in `build/screenshots/`, and the case tables and JSON files in `build/` are derived from
the MICCAI WMH Segmentation Challenge data:

> Kuijf, Hugo; Biesbroek, Matthijs; de Bresser, Jeroen; et al. "Data of the White Matter Hyperintensity (WMH) Segmentation
> Challenge", DataverseNL, https://doi.org/10.34894/AECRSD

They are shared under the licence of the source data, **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
(CC BY-NC 4.0)**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ : give credit as in the README (section "Data, credit and
licence", including the challenge paper Kuijf et al., IEEE TMI 2019, https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2019.2905770),
**non-commercial use only**.

Changes made: single axial FLAIR slices, cropped to the brain, reduced to 8 bit, together with the manual masks and the mask of
an algorithm (BIANCA) on these slices.

The code of this repository (index.html, build scripts) is under the MIT licence: see `../LICENSE`.
